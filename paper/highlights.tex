\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Simulated seeded ground truth favours its seed tracker by 0.049--0.160 IDF1
\item The advantage comes mainly from identity errors that reviewers leave unfixed
\item Seed-correlated errors reorder trackers more than random errors of the same size
\item Consensus seeding reduces the distortion but does not protect trackers left out
\item Union seeding helps little, and score brackets never contained the true score
\end{itemize}
